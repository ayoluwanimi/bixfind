# BIXFIND FLYER DESIGN PROMPT

## Design Concept
Create a modern, professional A4-sized flyer/infographic for Bixfind - Nigeria's Premier Service Marketplace.

---

## BRAND ELEMENTS

### Logo
- Logo text: "BIXFIND" in bold, modern sans-serif font
- Tagline below: "Your One-Stop Service Marketplace"
- Color scheme: Blue (#0066FF) and Purple (#8B5CF6) gradient accents with white text on dark sections
- Logo icon: Stylized "B" with service connection lines or a handshake icon

### Color Palette
- Primary: #0066FF (Bright Blue)
- Secondary: #8B5CF6 (Purple)
- Accent: #10B981 (Green for CTAs)
- Background Light: #F8FAFC
- Background Dark: #1E293B
- Text Dark: #1E293B
- Text Light: #FFFFFF
- Warning/Safety: #F59E0B (Amber)

---

## FLYER LAYOUT (A4 Portrait)

### SECTION 1: HEADER (Full Width)
- Dark blue background (#0066FF)
- BIXFIND logo prominently displayed
- Tagline: "Your One-Stop Service Marketplace"
- Subtitle: "Connect with verified professionals near you"
- Hero image: Happy customer and service provider shaking hands or pointing at phone

### SECTION 2: THE PROBLEM (Left Side)
Title: "Finding Trusted Services Shouldn't Be Hard"

Pain Points (with icons):
- 🔍 Scrolling endless Google results
- ⏰ Waiting hours for unavailable technicians
- 😰 No guarantee of quality or safety
- 💸 Overpaying for unreliable services

### SECTION 3: THE SOLUTION - HOW BIXFIND WORKS (Center)
Title: "How BixFind Works in 4 Simple Steps"

Flow Diagram with numbered circles:

**Step 1: SEARCH**
- Icon: Magnifying glass
- Text: "Search for any service you need"
- Sub: "Browse categories or use AI smart search"

**Step 2: COMPARE**
- Icon: List/Checklist
- Text: "Compare verified providers"
- Sub: "Check ratings, reviews & safety badges"

**Step 3: CONNECT**
- Icon: Phone/Chat bubble
- Text: "Contact directly via chat or call"
- Sub: "No middleman, deal directly"

**Step 4: GET SERVICE**
- Icon: Happy face/Thumbs up
- Text: "Get quality service delivered"
- Sub: "Rate your experience"

### SECTION 4: TRUST & SAFETY FEATURES (Right Side)
Title: "Your Safety is Our Priority"
Icon grid:

🛡️ **Verified Providers**
- ID verification
- Background checks
- Police clearance

✅ **Insurance Coverage**
- Basic & Full options
- Protected transactions

⭐ **Ratings & Reviews**
- Real customer feedback
- Trusted recommendations

📍 **Location Verified**
- GPS-tagged addresses
- Local providers

### SECTION 5: CATEGORIES (Bottom Grid)
Title: "Services for Every Need"

Category icons with labels in 2 rows:
Row 1:
- 🏠 Home Services (Plumbing, Electrical, Cleaning)
- 💻 Tech & IT (Web, App, Repairs)
- 🚗 Automotive (Repairs, Sales, Tracking)
- 💇 Beauty (Salon, Makeup, Spa)

Row 2:
- 🎵 Musicians (Singers, DJs, Producers)
- 🎓 Education (Tutors, Training, Classes)
- 🎉 Events (Planner, Catering, Photography)
- 🍔 Food & Catering (Meals, Snacks, Drinks)

### SECTION 6: FOR PROVIDERS (Call to Action)
Title: "Are You a Service Provider?"
- Green/Teal background
- Benefits list:
  - ✨ FREE mini-website creation
  - 📱 Mobile-optimized business page
  - 🎵 Music/portfolio showcase
  - 🛒 Online store & product listings
  - ⭐ Build your reputation
  - 💰 Accept payments directly
- CTA Button: "JOIN AS PROVIDER"

### SECTION 7: FOR CUSTOMERS (Call to Action)
Title: "Need a Service?"
- Blue background
- CTA: "DOWNLOAD APP" or "VISIT WEBSITE"
- Website: www.bixfind.com
- Phone: Available on all platforms

### SECTION 8: FOOTER
- Contact info: support@bixfind.com
- Social media icons: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp
- Copyright: © 2024 BixFind. All Rights Reserved.
- Tagline: "Connecting Nigeria, One Service at a Time"

---

## VISUAL STYLE
- Modern, clean, professional
- Nigerian-themed where appropriate (subtle)
- Gradient backgrounds for key sections
- Rounded corners on cards
- Soft shadows for depth
- Flat icons with slight color fills
- Professional stock photos (diverse Nigerian professionals)

---

## DESIGN TIPS FOR TOOLS LIKE CANVA/FIGMA
- Use 2480 x 3508 pixels (A4 at 300 DPI) or 1080 x 1920 for social media
- Export as PNG or PDF
- Include bleed marks if printing
- Use free fonts: Poppins, Montserrat, or Inter

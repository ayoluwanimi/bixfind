# Bixfind - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. **Access the Live Site**
```
https://bixfind.netlify.app
```

### 2. **Create an Account**
1. Click "Sign Up" button
2. Choose account type (Customer or Provider)
3. Fill in your details
4. Verify your email with the 6-digit code
5. ✅ Account created!

### 3. **Login**
1. Go to Sign In
2. Enter your email
3. Enter your password
4. ✅ Logged in!

---

## 👤 For Customers

### Browse Services
1. Go to Home page
2. Use search bar to find services
3. Filter by location
4. Browse service categories

### Book a Service
1. Find service you want
2. Click "Book Now"
3. Choose date and time
4. Complete payment
5. ✅ Service booked!

### Manage Dashboard
- **Orders:** View all your bookings
- **Wallet:** Add funds, track spending
- **Favorites:** Save services you like
- **Profile:** Update your information

---

## 🏪 For Service Providers

### Setup Your Account
1. Sign up as Provider
2. Complete verification
3. Go to Provider Dashboard
4. ✅ Ready to list services

### Add Services
1. Go to Services tab
2. Click "Add Service"
3. Fill in:
   - Service title
   - Description
   - Price
4. ✅ Service live!

### Manage Your Business
- **Services Tab:** Add, edit, delete services
- **Sales Tracking:** Monitor your earnings
- **Wallet:** Withdraw your money
- **Mini Website:** Create your provider profile

### Create Your Mini Website
1. Go to "Mini Website" tab
2. Fill in your bio
3. Upload cover image
4. Set business hours
5. Click "Publish"
6. ✅ Website live at: provider-{id}.bixfind.app

## ⚠️ Troubleshooting

### Can't Login?
- Check email and password
- Make sure you've verified your account
- Try resetting password

### Email Not Received?
- Check spam folder
- Verify email address is correct
- Try signing up again

### Service Not Showing?
- Make sure service is "Active"
- Check if you filled all fields
- Try refreshing page

### Payment Failed?
- Check wallet balance
- Verify payment method
- Contact support if issue persists
